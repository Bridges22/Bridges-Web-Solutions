## Frequently Asked Questions

### **Q: How long does it take to build a website in Kenya?**
A: Typical timelines range from 2-8 weeks depending on complexity. Basic business websites take 2-3 weeks, while e-commerce sites need 4-8 weeks.

### **Q: Do I need to provide content for my website?**
A: Most web design companies can help with content creation, but providing your own content often results in better, more authentic messaging.

### **Q: Will my website work on mobile phones?**
A: Yes, all professional web design companies in Kenya should provide mobile-responsive websites as standard.

### **Q: Can I update my website myself?**
A: Most modern websites include content management systems (CMS) that allow you to make basic updates yourself.

### **Q: What ongoing costs should I expect?**
A: Typical ongoing costs include hosting (KSh 5,000-15,000/year), domain renewal (KSh 1,500/year), and maintenance (KSh 10,000-30,000/year).
